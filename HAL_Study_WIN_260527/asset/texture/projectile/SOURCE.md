- Fireball: Free Pixel Effects Pack #13 - Fireballs
  - https://xyezawr.itch.io/free-pixel-effects-pack-13-fireballs
- Frost crystal: Free Pixel Effects Pack #14 - Mini Magick Shoots 4
  - https://xyezawr.itch.io/free-pixel-effects-pack-14-mini-magick-shoots-4
- Storm arrow: Free Pixel Effects Pack #15 - Magic Arrows
  - https://xyezawr.itch.io/gif-free-pixel-effects-pack-15-magick-arrows
- Venom comet: Free Pixel Effects Pack #17 - Mini Magick Shoots 3
  - https://xyezawr.itch.io/free-pixel-effects-pack-17-mini-magick-shoots-3
- Void orb: Free Pixel Effects Pack #19 - Mini Magick Shoots 4
  - https://xyezawr.itch.io/free-pixel-effects-pack-19-mini-magick-shoots-4

The author permits use in free and commercial projects and modification. Credit is not required but is appreciated. The original packs may not be redistributed or resold. These project sprites are cropped and, where needed, rotated copies of individual animation frames.

No generative AI was used for these source packs, according to their itch.io pages.
