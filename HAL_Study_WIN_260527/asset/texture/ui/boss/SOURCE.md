built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-08-24.

dark crimson and near-black diagonal hazard stripes and the exact repeated text
`DANGER DANGER DANGER`.

The in-game renderer crops the opaque banner region from the generated canvas
and repeats it horizontally for the boss entrance animation.
